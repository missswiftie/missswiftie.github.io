We propose UniETP, a unified interface that integrates four common simulators (AI2-THOR, VirtualHome, Habitat, and BEHAVIOR) into a consistent observation and action space, and automatically constructs a diverse, multi-difficulty dataset to support scalable training and evaluation of generalizable embodied task planning.
